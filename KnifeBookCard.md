# KnifeBookCard — a birth card for knives that don't have one

Not every knife comes with a maker's record card, and cards get lost. Fill
this in — every field is optional, write what you know — and your knife can
be cataloged just like one that shipped with its papers. Drop the filled-in
file (or a photo of your handwritten notes) next to the knife's photos and
the AI intake reads it like it reads a birth card.

> **This is a collector's own record, not a certificate of authenticity.**
> It documents what the owner knows about the knife — it proves nothing
> about origin, and it is not a maker's document.

Prefer pen and paper? Print `html/card.html`, hand-write it, and photograph
it with the knife.
